## The first 2 stages

I had a goal this year, as I said earlier, to get to 10k MRR

I worked on it today, as I did on many days of the year, the problem really comes down to consistency and not escaping the first 2 stages of indie development, making the product and then launching the product. I have really devolved this last year, and right now, it just looks like the right time to learn something new, the world is breaking apart, SaaS businesses are dying left and right. A new economy is taking shape,

## 2 things straight

Let me get 2 things straight for myself here:

1. I will work on what interests me, not by following what others teach, but by providing what other people want, I will have to go there and talk, I know that, I will not seek advice, I will genuinely be myself when I am out there, post, create content, however I wish it. Do, learn and Do again.
2. The people who do their job and are willing to grow, and persist, will grow in the AI era, thats what I think, so stop sobbing and start living life crazy,

## The age of tiny SaaS

But the age of tiny SaaS, from pdf tools, video downloaders, to habit trackers and invoice trackers, everything that is simple has come to an end, in the coming times even easier fixes will be fixed, you have to get out there and make some real value to the economy man,

Will google run out of business in 4 years? Yes? Then we are cooked.
No? then its all going to be fine.

## A billion problems

I think the economy is going to grow, but not all. Will AI solve food crisis? Will AI get people out of poverty? If its so good even now, why havent I built a billion dollar business? Cause I am lazy and fucking stupid to have guts and do unimaginable things, too naive to think that the world revolves around me, and that I need to act good in front of them.

There are still a billion problems to solve, more money than ever before will be printed, more value will be created from the same resource, so stick around, do your job and you will get your share accordingly.
